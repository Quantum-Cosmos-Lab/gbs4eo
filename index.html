gbs4eo interactive notes

Interactive pages that go with the Gaussian boson sampling code in this repository. Pick one to open it.

  • GBS pipeline walkthrough

    A graph followed through every step of the GBS finder: the adjacency matrix, the squeezing and interferometer derived from it, the photonic circuit, the photons the simulator returns, and how each photon pattern becomes a candidate subgraph and then a cluster.

    walkthrough.html

  • Beam splitter: truncated circuit vs. ideal optics

    Send a Fock state into the beam splitter and compare where the photons go in the 28-gate qubit circuit (cutoff 4, two qubits per mode) and in an ideal, untruncated beam splitter.

    truncatedBS.html
